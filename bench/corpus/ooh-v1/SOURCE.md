# OOH snapshot v1 — source and license

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, **Occupational Outlook Handbook**
(OOH), 2023–33 edition. https://www.bls.gov/ooh/

Snapshot: Internet Archive Wayback Machine captures nearest **2025-01-01**
(`https://web.archive.org/web/20250101000000id_/https://www.bls.gov/ooh/...`).
Per-page capture timestamps and original URLs are in `manifest.json`.

Contents: 48 occupation pages, each with the OOH Quick Facts, Summary sections,
Pay, Job Outlook, education requirements, and Similar Occupations table. The
text is extracted from the public HTML; no images or third-party assets.

License: OOH text is a U.S. Government work and is in the public domain in the
United States. BLS requests attribution; cite “U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics,
Occupational Outlook Handbook” and the snapshot date above. Some linked
third-party material is not included.

Caveat: this is a **frozen 2023–33 snapshot**. OOH numbers are estimates and
are updated by BLS; do not treat them as current. Questions must be answerable
from these pages, not from world knowledge or a later OOH edition.
