# Commercial Services Agreement

This is a synthetic contract for the formal-verification demo. It is not legal
advice and it is not a real agreement.

## 1. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## 2. Limitation of Liability

Supplier's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the 12 months
preceding the claim.

## 3. Notices

Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days' prior written notice.

## 4. Term and Renewal

The initial term is 12 months and renews for successive 12 month periods.

## 5. Data Protection

Supplier shall comply with applicable data protection laws.

## 6. Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party.
